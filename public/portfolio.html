01 — About

A developer who designs motion into the architecture.

/ 01

I'm Ari Vega — a creative developer with a soft spot for the space between interaction and art direction. For the last eight years I've been building the kind of websites that make people lean in: real-time graphics, scroll-driven narratives, and the small details that turn a product into an experience.

My work lives at the intersection of engineering and craft. I write shaders in the morning, refine easing curves in the afternoon, and care deeply about accessibility, performance budgets, and the texture of a well-built interface — the way a button responds, the rhythm of a reveal, the weight of a transition.

Currently independent, working with studios and product teams who want their web presence to feel alive. Previously at a few agencies you may have heard of, shipping award-adjacent work for brands in music, fashion, and technology.
